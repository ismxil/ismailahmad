About

I am a Designer, from Nigeria, currently based in Berlin. I am an entrepreneur by heart, with nearly a decade of experience working for startups, banks, and global organisations across Europe, Africa, and North America. I work at the intersection of design, technology, business and strategy, turning complex ideas into something simple, useful and purposeful.

Previously, I worked as a Senior Product Designer at Accenture Song, contributing to award-winning experiences recognised by Red Dot, iF Design, and MENA Design Awards. I've also led design at banks like Sterling Bank, shaping financial products used by millions of people in Nigeria and Europe.

I currently run Cadara Studio with friends, an independent design practice helping ambitious businesses build distinctive brands and digital products.

My approach is rooted in curiosity, clarity, and a belief that good design should make life simpler, create business value, and solve problems worth solving.

Beyond design, I'm a student of business, philosophy, film and science. I enjoy following my curiosity by writing, discovering new places, and bringing interesting people together to learn and share.

If you're building something in banking, logistics, hospitality, energy or another space that requires fresh perspective where design can make a positive difference, I'd love to talk.
