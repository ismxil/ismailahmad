# Mainland expansion

Built-in image generation created `lagos-mainland-v1.png` with `lagos-atlas-v3.png` as a style reference. The original waterfront source remains unchanged. The new section is rendered at x=-1200, y=-1700, width=3200, height=2133 in the existing atlas coordinate system. Its overlap with the old section is illustrative; streets are not georeferenced or guaranteed to join. District markers are placed from real coordinates (see below). The expanded coverage and forest envelope are independent from the source images, so future sections can be added without regenerating existing artwork.

## Final generation prompt

Create a new northern and western mainland expansion tile for the attached illustrated Lagos atlas. Reference image is STYLE ONLY; do not duplicate its lagoon bridges and waterfront towers. Landscape 3:2. Entire image a continuous dense Lagos mainland city viewed from steep 65-degree downward orthographic isometric map view, uniform building scale, no sky, no horizon. Same delicate ink outlines, Ghibli-inspired cel-painted small buildings, tropical greenery, sunlit cream walls, multicolored roofs, slate roads and yellow danfo buses as reference. Important main landmark: large Murtala Muhammed airport near centre-right with two long parallel north-south runways, detailed terminal aprons, small aircraft, hangars. Dense Agege and Ojokoro residential neighborhoods along upper left and upper middle, leafy Ikeja and office/commercial blocks upper right, Alimosho expansive dense residential neighborhoods across left half, markets and dense Mushin lower right, Surulere midrise blocks and a recognizable oval sports stadium lower centre-right. Western Ojo and Satellite Town neighborhood fabric lower left. A small working port at very bottom left with docks, cargo containers and just a narrow water strip. Almost all land, NOT lagoon-centered: 95 percent city blocks, street networks and greenery. Continuous city streets extend to all four edges so this can join an existing waterfront tile along bottom right. Hundreds of different small buildings and yards, real intricate map-like road detail, not repeated cubes. No labels, no text, no UI, no borders, no clouds, no cutout or isolated island, no bridge focal point. Very high detail suitable for zooming.

## Georeferenced places

Every place carries its real `lat`/`lng`, checked against Apple Maps (hybrid view centred on 6.505195, 3.344946), and its card links out to Apple Maps. Mainland pins are positioned from those coordinates by `project()` in `js/lagos.js`. The painting isn't surveyed and its airport is roughly twice life size, so each axis is mapped piecewise (`LNG_TO_X`, `LAT_TO_Y`): the real airport footprint lands on the painted airport, and districts keep their true north/south/east/west order around it. Waterfront pins stay hand-placed on their painted landmarks, because that tile is drawn at a different scale.

A future mainland repaint traced from Apple Maps should be laid out to match `project()`, so the pins stay correct without manual nudging.
