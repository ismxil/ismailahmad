# Forest surround

`forest-canopy-v1.png` was created with the built-in image generator. The city asset `lagos-atlas-v3.png` is unchanged. Rendering clips the city's outer edge to an artistic cutout and repeats the forest in world coordinates around it. This is a decorative coverage boundary, not an imported Google Earth boundary.
