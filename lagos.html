A love letter to the city

A city worth getting lost in.

From the mainland to the islands, this is a playful, explorable portrait of Lagos: its water, its energy, and the places that make it feel like home.

This AI-generated illustrated atlas is a stylized illustration of selected Lagos places. Buildings, distances, and landmark positions are artistic approximations, not navigation or survey data. The city is framed by a decorative forest surround and an artistic boundary, not a surveyed Lagos boundary. Zooming out reveals the whole cutout; zooming in uses the original city image, not newly generated street-level tiles. The mainland and waterfront are separate illustrated sections with approximate district placement. The atlas is designed to grow area by area; it does not yet cover every part of Lagos State.

Inspired by Isometric NYC, tiny.london, and your supplied animation and city-art references.
