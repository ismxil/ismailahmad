A love letter to the city

A city worth getting lost in.

From the mainland to the islands, Tiny Lagos is a playful, explorable portrait of Lagos: every street, building, park and stretch of water drawn from real map data, in the colours of the city.

The streets, buildings, water and names come from OpenStreetMap, mapped by thousands of volunteers. Building heights, roof colours, trees and traffic are illustrative. It covers metropolitan Lagos, from Ojo to the Lekki Peninsula and from the Atlantic to Ojokoro.

Inspired by Isometric NYC and tiny.london.
